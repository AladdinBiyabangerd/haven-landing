## Full Description

```
Heselo is a venue management SaaS for entertainment venues that sell time — PlayStation / console clubs, karaoke rooms, billiards halls, anti-cafés and VIP room lounges — primarily in Azerbaijan.

What it does:
• Book rooms, stations and tables
• Run live floor sessions (start, extend, settle)
• Cash shift / POS for the club floor
• Inventory and basic stock
• Customers, staff permissions and statistics
• Languages: Azerbaijani, English, Russian

Who it is for:
Venues whose main product is room or table time, not a full restaurant kitchen.

Who it is not for:
Heselo is not a restaurant kitchen POS. It does not replace systems built for KDS, recipes, dining-room service and delivery (for example iiko, Clopos or Dine when those are the right fit). If you need kitchen workflows, keep a restaurant POS; if you need booking + live sessions + cash for clubs, Heselo is the lighter option.

Pricing:
Subscription plans start from about 25 AZN per month depending on venue type and capacity. Free demo via the website contact form or WhatsApp — no card required.
```
